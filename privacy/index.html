Privacy policy

Quick Reminders · Effective 5 November 2026

In short: Quick Reminders does not collect, store or share any personal data on our servers. We don't have servers. Everything you set up stays on your phone. There are no ads, no analytics and no account.

Who we are

Quick Reminders is made by Inter6 Solutions ("we"). Questions about this policy: support@inter6solutions.com.

What stays on your device

Your reminders, schedules, sounds, vibration patterns, colours and other settings are stored only on your phone. We never receive them. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them.

Location

Quick Reminders does not use your location.

Notifications, alarms and vibration

The app uses notifications, exact alarms, the "start at boot" permission and vibration to deliver your reminders on time. None of these send any data off your device.

Purchases

Optional tips are processed by Google Play. Google handles your payment details under the Google Privacy Policy. The app only receives confirmation of what was purchased, so it can unlock the matching extras. We don't receive your name, email or payment details.

Backups

If you've turned on Android backup, Android may include the app's settings in your personal Google backup. That backup belongs to your Google account and is controlled by you. We have no access to it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there's nothing for us to access, correct or delete. All your data is on your device, under your control. If you have a question or a complaint, email us. If you're in the EU, you may also contact your local data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, for example because a new feature works differently, we'll update this page and the date above.
