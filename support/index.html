Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email support@inter6solutions.com. Tell us your phone model and Android version if something isn't working.
My reminders arrive late or not at all

Some phones put apps to sleep to save battery. In the app, open More and follow the battery tip, or go to Android settings → Apps → Quick Reminders → Battery and choose "Unrestricted". Also check that notifications and "Alarms & reminders" are allowed.

"While at" isn't working

It needs location access set to "Allow all the time" (Android settings → Apps → Quick Reminders → Permissions → Location). If your location can't be found, the reminder is delivered anyway, so you never miss one.

I bought a tip on another phone

Sign in with the same Google account and open the app. Purchases are restored automatically by Google Play.

How do I delete a reminder?

On the home screen, swipe the reminder to the left.

Does the app collect my data?

No. Everything stays on your phone. See the privacy policy.
